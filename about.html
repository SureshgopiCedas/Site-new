About Manage Automation

Tame risk. Build clarity. Anchor knowledge.

We help you finish on time, keep control in your own hands, and protect your investment long after commissioning.

Our story

Two decades inside automation projects.

Manage Automation is created by Cedas.dk, a Danish automation and engineering company. For more than 20 years we have worked with manufacturers across Europe, the U.S. and Asia, from first concept to final handover.

Again and again we saw the same pattern: projects that ran late, vendors that each worked their own way, and owners who could not access their own systems once the contractors left.

So we turned what works into a framework. It makes complex projects into repeatable success stories, where every vendor, engineer and operator follows the same playbook.

What we believe

When clarity flows downward, confidence moves upward.

The owner sets the target. Management controls the gates. Engineers follow one structure. Technicians build from tested parts. When that chain is clear, projects finish on time, and everyone wins.

Just clear frameworks, confident teams, and projects that finish on time.
